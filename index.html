Student Management System

A Student Management System (SMS) is a software application designed to help educational institutions manage and organize student information efficiently. It allows schools and colleges to register students, store personal details, manage course enrollment, record attendance, enter examination marks, calculate results, and generate academic reports. The system enables administrators, teachers, and students to access relevant information according to their roles and permissions. By automating administrative tasks, a Student Management System reduces paperwork, minimizes errors, saves time, improves data security, and makes it easier to retrieve and update student records. Overall, it improves the efficiency of school operations and supports better communication and decision-making within an educational institution.
